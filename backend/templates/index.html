👋 How to use this console

  1. Draw an area — use the ⬛ rectangle, ⭕ circle, or ✏️ polygon tool on the map (top-left)
  2. Adjust the sliders — how many mobile clinics (MMUs) and the target travel time
  3. Click ⚡ Run Spatial Optimization — the AI places clinics at real hospitals within your area
  4. Click any village/hospital — see details + send emergency services
  5. Click a ⭐ clinic — see disease data, medicine stock, and supply routes
